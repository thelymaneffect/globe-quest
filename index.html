How to play
  1. Choose the country (or state) you want to be, and pick your empire color.
  2. Tap any place on the map to learn about it, then press Challenge.
  3. The battle wheel starts split in half: a 50% chance for you and 50% for them.
  4. Brain Boost: answer two questions. Each right answer raises your chance of winning (50% → 75% → 87.5%). Each wrong answer raises theirs. Pick any topics you like: Capitals, Famous Landmarks and Geography. The questions are always about the place you’re battling, and the answers are on its card!
  5. Spin the wheel! If it lands on your color, you take over their whole empire. If it lands on theirs, they take over yours.
  6. If you lose, choose a new country to play as from the ones still standing.
  7. Own the whole map to win! Zoom in to see capitals ★, cities ●, rivers and mountains.

Your game saves automatically on this device. Use the ☰ menu any time to restart.
