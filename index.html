How to play
  1. Choose the country (or state) you want to be, and pick your empire color.
  2. Tap any place on the map to learn about it, then press Challenge.
  3. The battle wheel starts split in half: a 50% chance for you and 50% for them.
  4. Brain Boost: answer two questions. Each right answer raises your chance of winning (50% → 75% → 87.5%). Each wrong answer raises theirs. Pick any topics, listed easiest to hardest: Capitals, Where in the World, Famous Landmarks, Land & Nature and Flags. The questions are always about the place you’re battling, and the answers are on its card!
  5. Spin the wheel! If it lands on your color, you take over their whole empire.
  6. If it lands on theirs, it’s okay to feel disappointed. 🌱 Let’s learn from it and try again: study the card, then retry. Every try starts fresh at 50/50.
  7. No-loss mode (recommended for ages 4–9): losing a spin never costs anything. Real stakes (ages 7–9+): losing a spin costs your newest place, never your home. Kids with ADHD often need a few extra years, so the higher end of each range fits them.
  8. Own the whole map to win! Zoom in to see capitals ★, cities ●, rivers and mountains.

Your game saves automatically on this device. Use the ☰ menu any time to restart.

Types of Maps

The Earth is round, but paper and screens are flat. Every flat map has to stretch or squish the world a little, so mapmakers choose what to keep correct. Look for Greenland and Africa on each map and see how their sizes change!

💡 Did you know?Africa is about 14 times bigger than Greenland. Greenland is about 2.2 million square kilometers, and Africa is about 30 million. On the Mercator map they look almost the same size!
